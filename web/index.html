Turn off on-device AI.
Get your storage back.

Gemini, AICore and Galaxy AI can take up to 7 GB on a phone. This page turns them off over USB, right from your browser. Nothing is installed and nothing is uploaded. Every step can be undone.

  1. Turn on USB debugging.
    How? Settings → About phone → tap Build number 7 times. Then Settings → System → Developer options → turn on USB debugging. On Samsung, Build number is under About phone → Software information.
  2. Plug the phone into this computer with a USB cable.
  3. Click Connect, pick your phone, then tap Allow on the phone.
No phone at hand? Try the demo

This browser can't talk to USB devices. Use Chrome, Edge, Brave or Opera on a computer, or the command-line version: npx github:maximilianfeix/RemoveAndroidAI

Works on Android 10 and later. Nothing is uninstalled: apps are switched off with Android's own pm disable-user, so you can turn them back on here or in Settings → Apps.
